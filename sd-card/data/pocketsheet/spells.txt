@SILVERED SHELL
level: 1
school: transmutation
time: 1 bonus action
range: self
components: V, S, M
duration: 1 minute
You touch a weapon and coat it in otherworldly bile. For the duration it becomes moongilded, and while you hold it, it deals an extra 1d4 force damage on a hit.

Higher slots: the duration grows to 10 minutes with a 2nd level slot, 1 hour with 3rd, 8 hours with 4th and 24 hours with 5th or higher.

Material: an empty phantasm shell.
